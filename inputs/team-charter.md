# Team charter — MGMT 450 project team

## Purpose

Deliver a 12-minute client presentation with aligned financial and market assumptions.

## Roles

- **Rotating facilitator** each week (Jordan → Casey → …)
- **Slide owners** assigned by section, not by seniority

## Decision rules

- **Consensus** on outline and competitor focus; facilitator proposes, team amends
- Deadlines posted in Slack within 24 hours of meetings

## Meeting norms

- Cameras on when possible; notify team if audio-only
- Start with **blocker round-robin** (2 minutes each) — added 2026-10-20
- Stay on agenda; off-topic items go to "parking lot"

## Conflict

Address directly in meeting; escalate to instructor only after documented team attempt.
